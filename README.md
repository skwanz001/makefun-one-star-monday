Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Star for Monday](https://make.fun/c/u-clever-harbor-75-one-star-for-monday-9ead4a61): one line of plain text, a one-star review of Monday written as though Monday were a restaurant, at most 25 words.

`main.py` (Python 3.13 standard library only) writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`:

> One star. Monday seated me by the toilets, served my motivation cold, and charged me for the whole week.

19 words, 104 characters, one line.
